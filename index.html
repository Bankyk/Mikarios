About Us

Delicious Treats from the Heart of Osogbo

Makarios Edibles is a food business based in Osogbo, Osun State. Its offerings span cakes, pastries, parfaits, small chops and food trays, giving you treats for quiet personal moments, for sharing, and for celebrations.

The name itself comes from a Greek word meaning "blessed." That's a fitting thought for food that's shared and enjoyed in good company.
